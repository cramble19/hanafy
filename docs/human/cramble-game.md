# Cramble's Sunward Archive

Cramble has a separate healthy-habit adventure called **The Sunward Archive**.
It uses an original fantasy-fiction mood: old libraries, lanterns, mountain
roads, oaths, and quiet acts of courage.

## Opening the archive

Choose Cramble on the home screen to open the archive directly.

Below the daily archive quote, **How was today?** records one optional Heavy,
Low, Okay, Good, or Bright emotion for the 4 AM tracker day. It is editable,
neutral, reward-free, and stored only in Cramble's profile.

If the tracker has not started, **Begin the First Oath** starts Cramble's own saved record.
There is no preview mode and nothing is saved before the oath begins.

## The habit game

- **First Draught** — drink water after waking, every day.
- **Training Yard** — do one physically intensive activity at a safe level,
  every day.
- **Provisioner's Plate** — eat a proper meal with protein and something
  colorful, every day.
- **Evening Seal** — brush your teeth before bed, every day.
- **Sunward Tablet** — take the planned vitamin D tablet with a meal, every
  Sunday.
- Easy, medium, and hard quests earn 1, 2, and 3 renown.
- Cramble gets three **passes** each week for days when a quest is not practical.
- Skipping resolves the quest gently but earns no renown.

There are no random lessons or long studies in the built-in catalog. The bottom
**Add habit** button lets Cramble deliberately add a goal done once or several
times in any chosen number of days or weeks. This supports rhythms such as once
daily, once every 3 days, once weekly, twice daily, or 3 times in 10 days. He
supplies the name, clear completion description, and easy, medium, or hard
difficulty.

Each tap records +1, and several records can happen on the same day. **Undo one**
corrects a mistaken record. The 1, 2, or 3 renown arrives only when the full
period target is complete; partial progress earns no partial reward and carries
no penalty. Flexible-window habits report one result for the whole period, so an
unfinished Monday is not treated as a failure when the weekly target is met
later.
The full creation behavior is described in [Custom Habits](custom-habits.md).

The first chapter is **The First Oath**. It completes at both Rank 5 and 35
renown, then hints at the next chapter, **The Ember Road**.

## Chronicle lines and atmosphere

The Archive lines are original, concise reminders about discipline, recovery,
and becoming stronger. Some use epic-fantasy imagery; a few quietly suggest that
a changed road can still lead somewhere bright. They never name a breakup or
borrow quotations from existing fantasy worlds.

The everyday Archive uses a dark, warm dusk palette with brass and ember light.
A small sword-and-flame mark moves gently near the daily line. It becomes static
when the device asks for reduced motion, while the Observatory remains the
deepest night scene.

## Observatory and Ledger

The **Lantern Observatory** overlooks a scenic sunward road. A sword-bearing
knight and a fellow traveler begin near the same fire. As completed habits build
Cramble's renown and rank, the knight advances toward a brighter ridge while
both figures remain visible. The distance is a quiet picture of continuing
forward, not a separate game or a judgment about either traveler.

The **Ledger** shows all quests in a stable archive index. Open any quest to
choose the past 7, 30, or 90 days, or all time. The first chart gives one card to
each real goal window—one day for a daily habit, one week for a weekly goal, or
one rolling block for a 3-day or 10-day habit. Each card shows exactly how many
records were made and whether that whole target was met, unfinished, still open,
or passed.

A second calendar shows the exact days the habit happened. Repeated records on
one day display their count. Empty dates stay neutral, so a once-in-10-days goal
does not create nine false red misses. The summary also shows targets met, total
records, and average records per week. Open goals and passes do not lower the
targets-met percentage.

The Ledger celebrates a genuine run with **🔥 N combo** after two completed goal
windows. One isolated unfinished window does not erase an otherwise strong
rhythm. A quieter **🕯️ Rekindle** cue appears only after three goal windows in a
row went unfinished; a single completed window begins the comeback. Open goals,
partial progress, and passes are neutral. Today's tracker can show the positive
flame, but never the Rekindle cue.

## Separation from Hana

Cramble has his own quest catalog, start date, progress, local device cache,
database profile, save queue, Observatory, and Ledger. Opening or changing
Cramble does not reuse Hana's flower quests or alter her saved garden.

There is no account sign-in yet. Profile separation does not encrypt local data
or protect the database API.
